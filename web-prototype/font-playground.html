SSB64 character-select name font

Source: the names baked into the 12 character-select portrait tiles (MNPlayersPortraits, RGBA32). Tan face with a 1 px black outline, 7 px cap height (the soft glow under the names belongs to the portrait art, not the text). In every pair the top row is the original sprite pixels and the bottom row the font rendering the same string. Diffs compare 8-bit colour and alpha of every pixel; pixels covered by character art (hats, ears…) are unknown and shown dimmed in the diff; pixels outside every glyph box (art and its own dark shading) are not compared.

A–Z — existing (cut from the portraits) vs recreation (font glyph)

Letters marked synth never appear on the character-select screen and were built from parts of real glyphs (J from the JIGGLYPUFF tile with a standard-width stem, T = E bar + I stem, V = flipped A, W = flipped M, Z = E bars + diagonal, Q = O + tail). Isolated letters have no neighbour outline merging in, so a crop from a word can differ in the columns between letters; the per-name comparison below is the exact test.
